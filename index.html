🛰️ Road Safety Network

Real-time vehicle, pedestrian & emergency awareness

🚗 Vehicle Driver Radar view, zone alerts, nearby vehicle & pedestrian awareness 🚶 Pedestrian Get warned when a vehicle is approaching nearby 🚑 Emergency Vehicle Broadcast ambulance / fire engine priority to nearby drivers

This is a browser-based demo of V2X (vehicle-to-everything) safety concepts using GPS and the internet instead of dedicated short-range radio. Positions are shared only while a role page is open, and only with other people using this same site.

GPS-based awareness system · not a substitute for attentive driving
